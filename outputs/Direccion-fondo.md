# Fondo de pared y plantas colgantes · Fresh Slice

Composición nueva creada con la herramienta de imágenes integrada, inspirada en la idea de pared y enredaderas de la referencia y en la dirección natural de Biofresh. Las plantas nacen fuera del borde superior y caen hacia abajo por los laterales. Pared blanca de estuco, sombras grises y centro despejado. Se generaron dos encuadres independientes sin utilizar la fotografía de referencia como imagen de entrada.

## Prompts finales

### Horizontal

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: original full-screen photographic background for Fresh Slice, a premium natural ingredient game with Biofresh-inspired art direction.
Primary request: create a NEW original composition of fresh green trailing vines falling vertically from the TOP edge down a bright textured white wall. Premium natural-food campaign styling: clean, fresh, botanical, refined, airy. Natural greens from deep leaf green to fresh spring green, restrained and cohesive with a premium Biofresh aesthetic.
Composition invariant: EVERY vine originates beyond the TOP edge and hangs DOWN under gravity. Thin winding stems and small varied oval leaves form graceful vertical cascades in the outer edges. Stems taper downward. Nothing grows up from the bottom. No side-entering horizontal branches. No botanical elements along the bottom edge. This is a new composition, not a reproduction of any stock photograph.
Scene/backdrop: tactile bright WHITE stucco plaster wall viewed straight-on, fine raised sandy grains and tiny irregular pits visible across the empty wall. Clear but tasteful wall relief, neutral gray leaf and stem cast shadows to the lower right from soft daylight at upper left. Gentle broader gray shadow near top corners. White remains luminous and neutral, not beige.
Subject/style: beautiful realistic small-leaf trailing vines, natural fresh foliage with visible veins and subtle gloss, a few tiny lime-green new leaves. Editorial botanical/product photography with art-directed spacing, premium realism, believable stems and leaf scales. No illustration or plastic CGI.
Usability: leave the central 70% mostly clear white textured wall for moving food and UI. Keep an especially clean empty area at top center for the existing Biofresh logo. Only plants, wall and natural gray shadows.
Avoid: pots, floors, ceiling, people, food, flowers, fruit, symbols, text, logos, labels, watermarks, borders, dark vignette, horizontal spreading vines, lower-edge foliage, thick jungle plants, oversized leaves, copied stock-image composition.
Format: wide LANDSCAPE 16:9. A light canopy enters at the two top corners only, with irregular slender vines cascading down the far left edge to about 70% of image height and far right edge to about 45%. Most foliage within outer 12-16% of width, occasional tiny tapering tendrils. Keep the top-middle 40% of width clean. Natural asymmetric balance, modest varied leaf clusters rather than a dense rectangular frame. Bottom 20% is uninterrupted white wall.
```
